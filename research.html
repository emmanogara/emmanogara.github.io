  • Undergraduate Researcher Department of Mathematics, UNC Chapel Hill August 2025 - May 2026 Advisor: Dr. Jeremy Marzuola Conducted independent research on algorithm development for optimization of particle arrays in transparent films, as to obtain configurations with desired light scattering properties. Particularly, I contributed to the development of a MCMC-Gradient Descent pipeline, employing computionally efficient MCMC simulations using Born approximations for FOM computation, followed by more computationally expensive full Maxwell solves (using the CELES MATLAB solver) in a gradient descent search (refining MCMC candidates).
  • Undergraduate Research Assistant Department of Political Science, UNC Chapel Hill October 2024 - May 2026 Advisors: Dr. Bryce Hecht, Dr. Anna Bassi Assisted on Dr. Hecht's game-theoretic dissertation research. Acknowledged in final dissertation titled "Essays on Authoritarian Institutions." (2026).
  • Mathematics Directed Reading Program Mentee Department of Mathematics, UNC Chapel Hill Advisor: Alice Peng August 2024 - December 2024 Completed a directed reading project relating to nonlinear dynamics in neuroscience, under the guidance of a UNC Chapel Hill Mathematics graduate student. Particularly, I created MATLAB scripts to simulate neuronal firing in a the axon of the giant squid Loligo forbesi. This implementation relied on computing and analyzing the eigenvalues of fixed points of a reduced-order Hodgkin-Huxley model (with model parameters chosen based on real experimental data). Additionally, my simulations included bifurcation analysis by way of varying the input current parameter I.
