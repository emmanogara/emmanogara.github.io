  • Undergraduate Researcher
    Department of Mathematics, UNC Chapel Hill
    August 2025 - May 2026
    Advisor: Dr. Jeremy Marzuola
    Conducted independent research on algorithm development for optimization of particle arrays in transparent films, as to obtain configurations with desired light scattering properties. Particularly, I contributed to the development of a MCMC-Gradient Descent pipeline, employing computionally efficient MCMC simulations using Born approximations for FOM computation, followed by more computationally expensive full Maxwell solves (using the CELES MATLAB solver) in the gradient descent search (refining MCMC candidates).

  • Mathematics Directed Reading Program Mentee
    Department of Mathematics, UNC Chapel Hill
    August 2024 - December 2024
    Advisor: Alice Peng
    Completed a directed reading project relating to nonlinear dynamics in neuroscience, under the guidance of a UNC Chapel Hill Mathematics graduate student. Particularly, I created MATLAB scripts to simulate neuronal firing in the axon of the giant squid Loligo forbesi. This implementation relied on computing and analyzing the eigenvalues of fixed points of a reduced-order Hodgkin-Huxley model (with model parameters chosen based on real experimental data). Additionally, my simulations included bifurcation analysis by way of varying the input current parameter I.
